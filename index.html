About

Safety‑minded work. Practical execution.

Self-employed electrician · one pair of hands

I take electrical jobs from the first call to the final test, working directly for homes, shops and offices, and dealing with you myself.

I connect the problem with the fix: a clear quote, a clear plan, a neat finish and honest testing before I switch it on.

Every job comes with a walkthrough: you see the board, the points and the tests yourself, and what to watch for.
